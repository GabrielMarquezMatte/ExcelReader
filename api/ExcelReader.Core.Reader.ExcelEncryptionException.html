Namespace
ExcelReader.Core.Reader

Thrown when an encrypted workbook cannot be opened. Inspect Reason to decide what to do: only PasswordRequired and PasswordIncorrect are worth prompting a user about — the other two are terminal, and retrying with another password will never help.
