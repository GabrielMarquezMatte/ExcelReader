An open workbook of any supported format (XLSX, XLSB, XLS, CSV): its sheets and the state they share. Sheets are obtained from it and read independently.

Remarks

Thread safety: once opened, a workbook is safe to use from several threads: reading its metadata, obtaining sheets and creating enumerators. Each enumerator is used by one thread at a time; several enumerators of one workbook, including two over the same sheet, may run on different threads. The exception is a CSV over a non-seekable stream, which serves a single enumerator: a second one throws InvalidOperationException. Using a single enumerator from two threads at once is undefined behaviour and is not checked. A stream or buffer passed to a workbook must not be used, modified or disposed by the caller until the workbook and every enumerator obtained from it are disposed.

Disposing the workbook closes it to new sheets and enumerators. Its file, stream and pooled buffers are released when the last enumerator obtained from it is disposed.
