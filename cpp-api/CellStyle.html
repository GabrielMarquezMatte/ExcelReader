Struct CellStyle

A cell format for WriterHandle: number format, font, fill, alignment and borders.

A default-constructed style is the general style. Colors are 0xRRGGBB, or -1 for unset; any other negative or above 0xFFFFFF fails the call with XL_INVALID_ARGUMENT, as does an out-of-range enum, size, indent or rotation. Strings are borrowed: they must outlive the call they are passed to.
